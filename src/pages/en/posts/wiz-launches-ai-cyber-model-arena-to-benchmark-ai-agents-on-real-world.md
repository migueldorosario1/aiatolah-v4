Wiz has introduced the AI Cyber Model Arena, a real-world benchmark for AI agents in cybersecurity.
The benchmark is intended to measure how AI agents handle cybersecurity tasks as they occur in practice.
It focuses on evaluating AI agents in the cybersecurity domain specifically.
The effort comes from Wiz, a cloud security company, according to the announcement.
The arena is positioned as a benchmark for AI agents operating in real-world cybersecurity scenarios.
