AMD is boosting AI and LLM performance for Radeon iGPUs by as much as 18 to 23 percent with Linux 7.4, according to Phoronix.
The improvement applies to Radeon integrated graphics running on the Linux 7.4 kernel.
The gains target AI and large language model workloads.
Phoronix reported the performance figures in its coverage of the change.
The boost is tied specifically to the Linux 7.4 release.
