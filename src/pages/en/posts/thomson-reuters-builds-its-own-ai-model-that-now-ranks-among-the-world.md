Thomson Reuters has built its own artificial intelligence model that now ranks among the best in the world, the company announced.
The model was developed internally at Thomson Reuters rather than relying on outside providers.
Thomson Reuters said the model has achieved a ranking among the world's leading AI systems.
The company framed the development as a milestone in its own AI capabilities.
The announcement did not include additional technical details beyond the model's top-tier ranking.
