Measuring what medical AI can actually do is the focus of a Stanford HAI report. The work centers on how the capabilities of medical AI systems are assessed. The report comes from Stanford HAI, the university's Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence. It addresses the gap between claims about medical AI and what those systems can actually do. The piece is published under the headline Measuring What Medical AI Can Actually Do.
