The RTX 2080 Ti graphics card, released in 2018, can gain significant longevity for artificial intelligence and content creation tasks. A service offered by gpusolutions.net promises to double the model's original VRAM, taking it from 11 GB to 22 GB.

According to gpusolutions.net, the upgrade replaces all original 1 GB GDDR6 memory modules with 2 GB pieces. The process involves BGA rework (soldering of the new chips), VBIOS reconfiguration for full recognition of the 22 GB, thermal paste and thermal pad replacement, as well as stress testing with Furmark, 3DMark, and real-world scenarios.

With double the VRAM, the RTX 2080 Ti becomes capable of handling ultra-high resolution textures, heavy creative workloads, and memory-intensive AI applications. This can be especially useful for those running language models or neural networks locally, without needing to invest in a newer card.

The service is specific for RTX 2080 Ti cards with TU102 chip and compatible PCB layout. gpusolutions.net warns that BIOS modifications are mandatory for proper functioning. Additionally, overclocking may suffer small variations compared to the original behavior. Cards with severe corrosion, burned PCB, or poorly done previous repairs may be rejected.

The final cost depends on the availability of high-capacity memory modules, and replacement parts are quoted separately. If the upgrade is not feasible, the diagnostic/no-repair policy applies. The execution period is 12 days, which may vary depending on the repair queue.

The service warranty covers labor and replaced memory components for 90 days, but does not apply to unrelated failures or subsequent modifications.

For those still using the RTX 2080 Ti and needing more VRAM for AI or creation, this can be a more affordable alternative than changing cards. The upgrade transforms the model into a 'VRAM powerhouse', in the company's words.
