Apple Machine Learning Research has published a post on local and distributed large LLM inference with MLX and RDMA on Mac Studio. The work centers on running large language model inference on Mac Studio hardware. MLX, Apple's machine learning framework, is used for the inference work. RDMA networking is used to support the distributed setup. The post covers both local and distributed inference approaches. The research comes from Apple Machine Learning Research.
