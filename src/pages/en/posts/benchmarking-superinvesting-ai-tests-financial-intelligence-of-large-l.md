alphaXiv has published research evaluating financial intelligence in large language models through a benchmark called SuperInvesting AI.

The study benchmarks LLM engines on financial tasks rather than general reasoning alone.

SuperInvesting AI serves as the evaluation framework for comparing model performance in the financial domain.

The research examines how LLM engines handle financial intelligence as a distinct capability area.

The work is presented on alphaXiv, a platform for sharing and discussing research papers.
