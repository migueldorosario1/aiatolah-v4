Thomson Reuters has provided benchmarking data for a forthcoming large language model, according to Law.com.
The benchmarking data is intended for the LLM that is set to be released.
Law.com reported the development involving the legal information and technology company.
The report links Thomson Reuters to the preparation of the new model through the benchmarking data.
The source outlet for this story is Law.com.
