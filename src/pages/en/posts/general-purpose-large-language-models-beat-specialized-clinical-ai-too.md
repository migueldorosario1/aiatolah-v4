General-purpose large language models have outperformed specialized clinical AI tools on medical benchmarks, according to a report published by Nature.
The finding concerns benchmarks designed to test performance on medical tasks.
The comparison placed general-purpose large language models against AI tools built specifically for clinical use.
The specialized clinical tools were designed for medical applications, yet trailed the general-purpose models on those benchmarks.
The Nature report is the source of the comparison and its results.
