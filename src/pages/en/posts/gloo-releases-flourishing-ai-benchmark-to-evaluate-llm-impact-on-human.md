Gloo has released a Flourishing AI Benchmark aimed at evaluating the impact of large language models on human well-being, according to HPCwire.

The benchmark is designed to assess LLMs against measures of human flourishing rather than conventional performance metrics alone.

The release comes as developers and researchers seek ways to gauge how AI systems affect users beyond task accuracy.

HPCwire reported the news in its coverage of high-performance computing and AI developments.

The benchmark adds to a growing set of evaluation tools focused on the human impact of AI models.
