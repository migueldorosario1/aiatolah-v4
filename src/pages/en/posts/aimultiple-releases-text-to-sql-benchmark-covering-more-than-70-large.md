AIMultiple has published a text-to-SQL benchmark that measures SQL accuracy across more than 70 large language models.

The benchmark focuses on the text-to-SQL task, in which a model translates natural language into SQL queries.

Its stated metric is SQL accuracy, evaluated across the full set of 70-plus LLMs included in the comparison.

The result is a comparative view of how different language models perform on the same text-to-SQL evaluation.

AIMultiple is the outlet behind the benchmark and its accompanying coverage.
