Crun AI announced the launch of an AI leaderboard designed to compare LLM, video, image, and audio models side by side.

The leaderboard places multiple model categories in one comparison view rather than separating them by modality.

According to the announcement, the tool covers large language models as well as video, image, and audio models.

The launch was reported by PR Underground.

The leaderboard is positioned as a way to evaluate models from different categories against one another in a single place.
