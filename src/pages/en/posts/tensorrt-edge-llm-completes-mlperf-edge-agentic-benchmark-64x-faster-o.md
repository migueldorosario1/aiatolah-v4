NVIDIA Developer reports that TensorRT Edge-LLM has completed the MLPerf Edge agentic benchmark 6.4x faster on Jetson AGX Thor.

The result was published by NVIDIA Developer, which covers the TensorRT Edge-LLM work on its developer news channel.

The benchmark in question is the MLPerf Edge agentic benchmark, according to the source headline.

The speed comparison in the report is 6.4x, measured on the Jetson AGX Thor platform.

TensorRT Edge-LLM is the software named in the report as completing the benchmark run.
