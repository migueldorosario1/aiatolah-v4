Databricks has published work on benchmarking coding agents against its multi-million line codebase.

The effort targets agents that write and modify code within a codebase of that scale.

Benchmarking at this size tests how agents handle a large production repository rather than small sample projects.

Databricks is known for its data and AI platform, and the codebase in question is its own.

The source does not disclose specific scores, model names, or numeric results.
