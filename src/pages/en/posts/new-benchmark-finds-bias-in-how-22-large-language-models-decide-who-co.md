A new benchmark has shown bias across 22 large language models in how they determine who qualifies as an expert.

The benchmark tested the models on their judgments of expertise, according to Tech Xplore.

The results indicate the bias appears across the full set of 22 LLMs evaluated.

The findings point to a pattern in how these systems assess expertise rather than an isolated case.

The benchmark adds a measurable test for bias in expert identification across many models at once.
