AIMultiple has published a benchmark of 25 embedding models for retrieval.
The benchmark focuses on how embedding models perform in retrieval.
The report covers 25 models rather than a single architecture.
Retrieval is the task the benchmark evaluates the models against.
AIMultiple released the findings as part of its research coverage.
