The National Institute of Standards and Technology has published a new report titled Expanding the AI Evaluation Toolbox with Statistical Models.
The report addresses the evaluation of artificial intelligence systems.
It focuses on statistical models as an addition to existing evaluation methods.
NIST is a federal agency within the United States Department of Commerce.
The report is available through the agency's official website.
